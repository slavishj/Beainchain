How to Play

Invite a friend

Tap Create a challenge. Then send your friend the link.

My Games
→Create a challenge
Your challenge
→Text invitation

Your friend taps the link to join. You can also use Copy invitation link to share it.

Start your turn

In My Games, find a game that says Your turn.

Game 1 · Your turn
→Open game
Your turn
→GO!

Tap Open game, then GO! to see your clue.

Connect the words

Find a word that goes after RAIN. It starts with B and has three letters.

Start with
RAIN
↓
B _ _
BOW→GUESS

Type BOW and tap GUESS. RAIN + BOW = RAINBOW.

Try again if you miss

Get it on your first try and earn 100 points. Each miss shows another letter and costs 20 points.

B _ _Worth 100 points
↓ You guessed BAG
B O _Worth 80 points

Guess BOW now and earn 80 points.

Points stop at 0. On a seven-letter word, five misses leave one blank. You can still guess, but the word is worth 0 points.

If the last letter is revealed, your turn ends automatically at 0 points.

Finish the chain

Your friend gets a turn on the same word. Then you both move on to the next word.

You340 points
Friend300 points
You win!

After four words each, the higher total wins. Each player can earn up to 400 points per chain.
