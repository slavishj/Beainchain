How to play

Build a chain of words, where neighboring words make a familiar phrase. Example: FIRE → TRUCK → STOP → SIGN (fire truck, truck stop, stop sign).

Each turn has its own countdown. Enter the exact missing word and tap Guess. A correct answer reveals the link and lets that team keep playing with a fresh timer. A wrong answer or timeout ends the turn immediately. The next team takes over the same chain.

The team completing the final link wins that round. All other teams receive a loss for the round. First to the configured number of round wins takes the match. You can keep playing additional matches in the same session.

The game runs locally on one device. Scores are saved in this browser. Sound is best enabled by interacting with the game before the timer expires.
