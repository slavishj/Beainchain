How to play

1. Pass the phone. Tap START to reveal the round’s chain. Every team gets the same words.

2. Guess the missing word before time runs out.

3. Wrong or out of time? Another letter appears. Tap CONTINUE to pass the phone (or try again in Solo).

4. Finish the chain. Fewest misses wins the round!

Example: FIRE → TRUCK → STOP → SIGN.
